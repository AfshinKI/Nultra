Nultra 0.6.0 adds a native Android app alongside the iPhone app.

- Android 8.0 and newer: direct phone BLE control, no Pi dependency.
- Compact two-column switch cards with real saved names, broadcast power/battery, signal bars, blue/gray presence, and glowing yellow bulbs.
- One brief name lookup for new switches; power and rename connect, authenticate, verify, and disconnect. Up to three transactions run concurrently.
- All Off / All On, Android Keystore-protected master password, and demo mode.


Physical Android BLE behavior and Samsung installation require on-device acceptance. No Google Play developer account is needed for this private APK.
